# Logging and Data Management Methods

Use these methods to manage log levels and other data, such as ledgers.

- can_deleteAllow online deletion of ledgers up to a specific ledger.
- ledger_cleanerConfigure the ledger cleaner service to check for corrupted data.
- ledger_requestQuery peer servers for a specific ledger version.
- log_levelGet or modify log verbosity.
- logrotateReopen the log file.
