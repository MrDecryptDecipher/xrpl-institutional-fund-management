# Use Tokens

Create and trade tokens (fungible or otherwise) in the XRP Ledger.

- Issue a Fungible TokenCreate your own token and issue it on the XRP Ledger Testnet.
- Trade in the Decentralized ExchangeBuy or sell fungible tokens for each other or for XRP in the decentralized exchange.
- Enable No FreezePermanently give up your account's ability to freeze tokens it issues.
- Enact Global FreezeFreeze all tokens issued by your address.
- Freeze a Trust LineFreeze an individual holder of a token.
- Create an Automated Market MakerSet up an Automated Market Maker (AMM)
